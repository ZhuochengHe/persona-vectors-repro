## Question and source

Does E01 vector injection increase the target trait, and which layer has the highest trait expression at a fixed coefficient? Follow paper §3.2, Figure 3 and Appendix B.4. Coherence is a separately reported outcome, never a layer-selection exclusion.

## Frozen settings

- Qwen3.5-4B base model, nonthinking, SDPA; frozen E01 raw vectors, without unit normalization for steering.
- Three traits; five development questions per trait and four rollouts, with exact question IDs and paired seeds held fixed across conditions.
- Scan one-based layers `{4,8,12,14,15,16,17,18,19,20,21,22,24,26,28,32}` for each alpha in `{1,1.5,2}`. Reuse one zero-hook baseline per trait.
- Injection is `h_l += alpha * raw_vector_l` at the final prefill position and subsequent decoding positions. Record vector norms and generation tokens. No finetuning.
- Luna scores trait and coherence using original rubrics, without calibration. Refusals/invalid judgments remain explicit; numeric trait means exclude them, with counts reported.

## Selection and validation

For each trait and each fixed alpha independently, select the layer with the greatest absolute mean development trait score. Break ties by lower layer number. Neither coherence loss nor paired gain is a selection gate. Do not stop an alpha sweep because another alpha has poor coherence.

Report three layer-score curves and three best-layer results per trait, with coherence curves alongside. Alpha=1 is predeclared as the primary layer choice for downstream monitoring; alpha=1.5 and 2 supply sensitivity results. B.4 specifies fixed-alpha selection but does not uniquely identify the alpha behind the reported layer numbers; the primary-alpha convention is our explicit adaptation, not a claim about author settings.

After development-only selection, evaluate the selected setting for each alpha on the frozen ten held-out questions, four rollouts each, plus one shared zero baseline per trait. Report all effects and quality losses; do not reject or relabel layers using held-out coherence.
